✓ Snapshot

Memory Town Stories is built for relaxed play on supported iPhone and iPad models. The experience is designed around short sessions, colorful boards, and optional progression features that can work fully offline once installed.

App Store labeling for this title currently states that no data is collected in the categories Apple asks developers to disclose. Labels can change with future updates—check the latest App Privacy section on the store page before you install or update.

◇ What may stay on your device

Like many casual games, progress such as unlocked worlds, cosmetic items, challenge history, or similar preferences may be stored locally so the app remembers where you left off. That kind of information normally remains on the handset or tablet unless a future version introduces optional cloud features—if that ever happens, release notes and store disclosures should describe it clearly.

Removing the app from your device typically deletes local app data along with it, subject to how your operating system handles offloading and backups.

◎ Apple platforms

Downloading, updating, or restoring purchases happens through Apple’s systems. Apple may process identifiers, receipts, and device information under its own agreements and privacy policy. Screen Time, Family Sharing, and system-wide privacy controls are managed entirely by Apple.

★ Young players

The game is listed for ages 4+ on the App Store. Caregivers can use Apple’s parental tools to approve installs, limit purchases, and shape how much time is spent in apps. This page is meant to be read together with a grown-up when it helps.

↻ Keeping this page current

Whenever gameplay or platform rules shift in a way that affects privacy expectations, this overview will be revised and the Effective date at the top will move forward. Opening the latest build from the App Store remains the most reliable way to confirm what you are installing.
