Memory Town Stories

Logic-pair memory adventure

Free Casual iPhone & iPad 4+

Flip lively cards, clear clever boards, collect stickers, and take on fresh goals every day. Memory Town Stories is a bright cartoon memory game where classic matching grows into something smarter: living cards react when you nail a pair, logic-pair levels link related ideas instead of identical pictures, and playful board events keep every round surprising.

Featured gameplay

  • Living cards — wiggle, bounce, and celebrate the right match.
  • Logic pairs — connect ideas like sun + shades or bee + flower.
  • Board events — wind, sleepy, sticky, sparkle joker, and trick twists.
  • Memory powers — peek, freeze, soft shuffle, and trace hint when boards get tough.
  • Collect & decorate — stickers, card backs, and your own Memory Room.
  • Daily challenge & relax mode — rotating goals or calm, timer-free play.

Story worlds to explore

  • Candy Park
  • Ocean Friends
  • Jungle Party
  • Toy Room
  • Space Picnic
  • Magic Kitchen

Jump into story worlds, daily challenges, quick play, or slow down in relax mode. Family-friendly art, smooth flips, and portrait-friendly design make it easy for kids and satisfying for adults who love memory puzzles and light collectible progression.
